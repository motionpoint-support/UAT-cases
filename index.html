UAT Cases: Route, Don’t Fix

What the UAT period is, how to spot a UAT case, and your three steps: milestone, customer note, assign. No troubleshooting, no billing. About 6 minutes, with background music.

0:00

UAT kit

Spot it: the orange banner on the case, “This case may be a part of a project’s UAT phase”, and the MPD email with the UAT period and assigned team. · Your 3 steps: complete the milestone, acknowledge the customer with a customer-visible note, assign to the team the MPD email names (QA, TAM, PE or PM). That’s it. · Never: troubleshoot, assign to the Delivery Team, bill time, or discuss fixes or dates with the customer. The PM handles all customer communication. · MPD Notes: “MTPE during the IC; Brand Adaptive once in maintenance” means the Initial Conversion uses Machine Translation Post-Editing (machine draft, human-reviewed), and new or updated content in maintenance uses Brand Adaptive (machine translation adapted to the brand’s voice and terms). · In doubt? Reach out to the project’s PM.
